02 / Unpublished manuscript · Summer 2026

From aerial video.
To parking-rule alerts.

Finding a vehicle is one part of the problem. Understanding where it stops, and for how long, brings the scene into context.

Detection, tracking, and context.

A research prototype combining YOLO11n detection, ByteTrack tracking, stationary-state analysis, and polygon-based parking rules.

Detect→Track→Analyze stops→Apply rules

Supervised by Md Nurul Amin.
Unpublished manuscript · University of Minnesota Crookston.

0.757Vehicle detection mAP@0.5
31Validation images

Fine-tuned on 123 training images using CPU-only hardware. The metric above evaluates vehicle detection.

Evaluation scopeAlert logic was compared with a MOG2 baseline in two controlled demonstrations. Real-world positive-event labels were unavailable, so these demonstrations do not establish real-world parking-alert accuracy.
Method & evaluation notes +

My contribution. Developed the prototype and fine-tuned YOLO11n on aerial vehicle imagery using CPU-only hardware. Combined tracking, stationary-state analysis, and spatial rules to explore parking alerts.

Detection evaluation. The reported 0.757 mAP@0.5 is vehicle-detection performance on 31 validation images following fine-tuning on 123 training images. It is separate from the alert logic.

Limitations. Two controlled demonstrations supported a comparison with MOG2. Missing real-world positive-event labels limit conclusions about parking-alert performance. The manuscript is unpublished.
